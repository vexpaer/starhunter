00 Why we exist

Most people buy a telescope once, look at the Moon twice, and put it in a closet. We think that's a design problem — so we are exploring instruments that are quick to set up, honest about what they show, and designed to last.

01 The telescopes

Three concepts. One direction: better nights outside.

We are exploring three possible telescope designs. Configurations and specifications below are illustrative, not finalized products or offers for sale.

SH-L72Grab-and-go

Lumen 72 Refractor

A compact 72 mm refractor idea for travel and quick setup. The optical configuration is a design target, not a tested product.

  • Aperture72 mm · f/6
  • OpticsFPL-53 air-spaced doublet

Meridian 130 Smart Newtonian

A 130 mm smart Newtonian concept with proposed plate-solving, object tracking and a traditional eyepiece.

05 About StarHunter

Two people. No outside funding.

StarHunter is an independent, two-person project focused on exploring more approachable telescope designs.

We have not raised outside investment. We are a small team developing ideas rather than a large company with a finished product line.

Our goal is simpler setup, understandable specifications and equipment that makes more nights under the stars possible. The telescope models on this page are concepts, not confirmed releases.
